About Wayfarer

We help you decide, not just dream.

Wayfarer started as a shared spreadsheet of favorite beaches, temples, and countries passed between three friends who travel constantly and plan terribly. It's now a small guide built to shorten the gap between "we should go somewhere" and an actual itinerary.

Our mission

Most travel sites bury good recommendations under ads and sponsored listings. Wayfarer does one thing: it shows real destinations, grouped simply, with enough photos and context to help you shortlist a trip in minutes instead of hours.

What we stand for

  • Honest picks. Every destination on Wayfarer is somewhere our team has actually researched or visited.
  • No noise. No pop-ups, no sponsored placements — just a search box and a list.
  • Always improving. We add new beaches, temples, and countries as our own trip list grows.
